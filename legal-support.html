2. What we provide

We monitor public tender sources, including the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets Authority (PPDA) and related government procurement channels, and deliver summarised alerts to subscribers via WhatsApp, based on the sector(s) and location(s) they select.

Tender information is sourced from public government channels. We summarise and deliver this information for convenience — we do not control, issue, or guarantee the accuracy of the original tender notices published by government or other entities.

Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Free trial

New subscribers receive a free trial period (typically two weeks) before any payment is required. We encourage you to use this period fully to evaluate the service before subscribing.

2. Monthly subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance. Because the service (tender alerts) is delivered continuously and immediately from the start of each billing period, payments are generally non-refundable once a billing period has begun.

3. When a refund may be considered

  • You were charged in error (e.g. duplicate payment, incorrect amount)
  • A technical failure on our side meant you received no alerts at all during the billing period
  • You cancel within 48 hours of a renewal charge and have not received any alerts in that new period

Refund requests in these cases are reviewed individually and, if approved, processed back to the original mobile money account within 7–10 business days.

4. What isn't covered

We don't offer refunds simply because no tenders matched your sector/location during a given period (tender volume varies naturally), or because you did not personally win a contract — we provide alerts and information, not a guarantee of results.

5. How to request a refund

Send a message through our Support page describing the issue. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
